Privacy

Last updated 16 October 2026

MyDay is a simple daily to-do app made by Kasah Kod. This page explains, in plain words, what it keeps about you.

If you don't sign in

Your tasks and notes stay on your device only. Nothing is sent to us.

If you create an account

We keep:

  • your email address;
  • your password, scrambled, never as you typed it;
  • the tasks and notes you write in MyDay, so they appear on all your devices;
  • a sign-in record (the date, your browser and its internet address), so you stay signed in.

Where it's kept

On Cloudflare's servers, in their Asia-Pacific region. Only MyDay uses it.

Emails we send

We use Resend only to send account emails: the link to confirm your email address, and the link to reset your password. Resend only sees your email address and the email we send you. We don't send newsletters or marketing.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking, no analytics. We never sell or share your information. MyDay uses one cookie, only to keep you signed in.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account yourself: open Settings, then Delete my account. It removes your account and everything stored online straight away, and clears the device you do it on. You can also email hello@kasahkod.cc from the address you sign in with, and we'll delete it within 30 days.

Signing out removes you from that device; your tasks already on it stay there until you clear them.

Questions
