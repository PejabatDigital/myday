Privacy

Last updated 14 October 2026

MyDay is a simple daily to-do app made by Kasah Kod. This page explains, in plain words, what it keeps about you.

If you don't sign in

Your tasks and notes stay on your device only. Nothing is sent to us.

If you sign in with Google

We keep:

  • your name, email address and profile picture, which Google shares when you sign in;
  • the tasks and notes you write in MyDay, so they appear on all your devices;
  • a sign-in record (the date, your browser and its internet address), so you stay signed in.

We only ask Google for your name, email address and picture. MyDay can't see your Gmail, Drive, contacts or anything else in your Google account.

Where it's kept

On Cloudflare's servers, in their Asia-Pacific region. Only MyDay uses it.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking, no analytics. We never sell or share your information. MyDay uses one cookie, only to keep you signed in.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and everything stored with it, email CONTACT_EMAIL_PENDING from the address you sign in with. We'll delete it within 30 days and let you know when it's done. Signing out removes you from that device; your tasks already on it stay there until you clear them.
